Coaching close-out

A one-page summary, in your words, for the conversation with your sponsor.

Answer the six questions below and the page builds a one-page PDF you can take into the meeting. You present it, not your coach.

Nothing here is sent anywhere. Your answers stay in this browser on your own machine, and your coach never sees them unless you choose to share them. You decide what goes in and what stays out. Anything you leave blank is simply left off the page.

Keep answers short. The limits are there so it all fits on one page.

1. Where I started

What did you want to work on when this began?

2. What I actually worked on

Often it turns out to be something different. If it changed, say so.

3. One thing I did differently

A specific situation. What was happening, what you did, and what came of it. One real example lands harder than any general description.

4. What the people around me would notice

What would your team or your colleagues say is different?

5. What I'm still working on

Where you've got to, and what's still live.

6. What I'd like from you

Addressed to your sponsor. What would you like them to notice, back, or do differently over the next six months? Be specific enough that they could say yes to it.
